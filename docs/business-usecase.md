## Business Use Case — Seer Varisai Thattu

We are a newly established business providing beautifully arranged **Seer Varisai Thattu (traditional ceremonial gift trays)** for weddings and other important occasions, built on hands-on experience preparing trays for marriage functions and receptions.

> **Positioning note.** This is a new business with no trading history under this name. The website must not claim years of experience, a number of events served, a team of artisans, or family heritage. It must not carry customer testimonials until real ones exist. See `docs/sprint/plan.md` Sprint 10 and the launch audit in the repo history.

We create and arrange customized gift trays containing items such as fruits, sweets, flowers, clothing, cosmetics, traditional gifts, and other decorative items based on the customer's requirements and budget.

Our customers are mainly families who are preparing for occasions such as **weddings, engagements, baby showers, seemantham, housewarming ceremonies, and other traditional celebrations**.

The main purpose of the website is to showcase our collections, display examples of our previous work, help customers understand our services, and allow them to easily contact us for enquiries and customized orders.

Customers should be able to browse different types of Seer Varisai arrangements, view detailed photos and information, understand the customization options, and request an arrangement based on their occasion, number of trays, preferred style, requirements, and budget.

The website should represent the business as a **trustworthy, premium and traditional brand**, while providing a modern and easy-to-use experience for customers. Trust should be built on the real photographs of completed work and on clear, verifiable statements — never on invented reviews, heritage or guarantees.
