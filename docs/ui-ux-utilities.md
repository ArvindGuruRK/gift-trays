## Website UI/UX Design Prompt

Design a premium, elegant, and visually impressive website for a family-run **Seer Varisai Thattu** business.

The website should combine **traditional South Indian wedding aesthetics with a modern luxury website experience**. It should feel warm, authentic, elegant, trustworthy, and premium rather than looking like a generic e-commerce website.

Use a sophisticated visual style with an **ivory/cream base, subtle gold accents, deep maroon or burgundy tones, warm earthy colors, elegant typography, generous whitespace, and beautiful high-quality imagery** of Seer Varisai Thattu arrangements, flowers, fruits, sweets, traditional gifts, and wedding decorations.

The homepage should have a strong cinematic hero section that immediately communicates the essence of the business. Use large visual imagery or video, elegant typography, subtle text-reveal animations, smooth scrolling, image transitions, and tasteful hover effects.

The overall experience should feel **premium and immersive**, with animations used to enhance storytelling rather than distract the user. Use smooth scroll-based reveals, subtle parallax effects, image zooms, elegant page transitions, and sophisticated micro-interactions.

The website should include sections for:

* Hero / Introduction
* Our Collections
* Featured Seer Varisai Arrangements
* Occasions We Serve
* Customization Options
* How Our Service Works
* Previous Work / Gallery
* About Our Family Business
* Customer Testimonials
* Contact / Enquiry
* Final Call-to-Action

The product and collection sections should be highly visual, allowing customers to explore different arrangements through beautiful photography.

Include a **custom enquiry experience** where customers can provide details such as their occasion, required number of trays, preferred style, requirements, event date, location, and approximate budget.

The website should be fully responsive and provide an excellent experience on **mobile, tablet, and desktop**.

The design should prioritize:

* Premium visual storytelling
* Strong typography
* Beautiful photography
* Smooth animations
* Clear navigation
* Easy-to-understand information
* Strong call-to-action buttons
* Fast and intuitive user experience
* Accessibility
* Mobile-first responsiveness

Avoid excessive gradients, excessive glassmorphism, overly flashy animations, generic SaaS layouts, or designs that make the website look artificial or AI-generated.

The final result should feel like a **premium boutique Indian wedding brand** that combines the beauty of traditional Tamil culture with a modern digital experience.
